The Anti-Gravity Codex // Axiom I

The Conservation of Gravity: Separating Physics from Fiction

True physical levitation is not the whimsical negation of gravity, but the precise balance of opposing fundamental vectors. Science fiction often treats “anti-gravity” as an elusive energetic field that switches off gravitational attraction. In rigorous physics, no such gravitational dipole has ever been observed. Instead, science achieves mass suspension through three rigorous physical pathways: macroscopic quantum trapping via flux pinning in Type-II superconductors, radiation pressure generated by high-intensity acoustic and optical standing nodes, and exotic solutions to Einstein’s field equations that deform the spacetime metric itself. Earnshaw’s Theorem proves that static magnetic fields cannot stably suspend a ferromagnetic object; thus, all stable equilibrium requires either quantum phase transitions, dynamic electrodynamic feedback, or diamagnetic field repulsion. This archive is devoted entirely to verifiable field mechanics, separating peer-reviewed physical breakthroughs from sensationalist folklore.
